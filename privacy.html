This page explains what LogicManse Study uses and what information is processed when you use the site.

Updated October 2026

Google sign-in and Firebase

Course access is invite-only. Google sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. If you are approved for a course, Firebase Firestore stores account-linked course information such as saved progress and practice results so they can sync across devices. The administrator who invites you manages your course access. Removing access does not automatically erase saved course records. To request access removal or deletion of saved records, contact the teacher or LogicManse administrator who invited you.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is used only for basic product improvement and loads only after you choose “Allow analytics.” Google Analytics is not anonymous: it may use a first-party client ID and collect browser/device details and approximate location. The site sends fixed page and course labels and a small set of study-tool events; its code is designed not to send names, email addresses, answers, scores, or free-text content.

Google Signals and Enhanced Measurement are off for this property, ad personalization is disabled in all regions, and email redaction is active. Google Analytics user and event data retention is set to two months. Analytics cookies are configured to expire after 60 days. You can change your choice at any time below.

Optional Desmos calculators

Some MHF4U study resources link to Desmos. The calculator is optional; opening it takes the browser to Desmos, which receives a normal request to its site. You can continue studying without opening Desmos. The app does not attach your Google account or answer text to the calculator link.

AI features

This version of LogicManse Study does not send student questions or answers to Gemini or another AI tutor.

Fonts and external services

The site uses system fonts; it does not load Google Fonts. The LogicManse logo image is served from logicmanse.ca. When you follow an external service link, your browser contacts that provider directly and its privacy terms apply.

Website hosting

The site pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. When your browser requests a page, GitHub receives standard connection information needed to deliver it, such as your IP address and browser details. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
