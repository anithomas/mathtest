How studying here works

The same four-step loop runs through every lesson, in every course

1

Learn it

A clear explanation plus a fully worked example — see the reasoning, not just the answer.

2

Check yourself

Low-stakes practice with instant, specific feedback — mistakes become part of the lesson.

3

Target weak spots

Review flagged topics before moving on, so gaps don't carry into the next unit.

4

Simulate the real thing

Timed, exam-style practice that matches the real assessment's tools and rules.
